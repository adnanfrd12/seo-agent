Overview: what we need and why

Three Google services feed your dashboard. Two take a few minutes; one needs Google's approval.

Your SEO dashboard reads performance data directly from Google, so every figure in your monthly report comes from the original source and is never estimated. To do that, we need permission to read your data. We never change your website, your listings or your settings through these connections.

What you'll need before you start

  • Access to the Google account that owns or administers each service (Analytics, Search Console, Business Profile).
  • The email address we'll send you for granting access (shown as [agency email] in this guide).
  • For Business Profile: a profile that is verified and has been active for at least 60 days, with your website listed on it.
Why Business Profile takes longer

Unlike Analytics and Search Console, Google only allows Business Profile data to be read by software after it has reviewed and approved an access request. Part C walks through that request. Everything else can be connected the same day.

Google Analytics 4

Give us read access to your GA4 property.

  1. Open Google Analytics

    Go to analytics.google.com and sign in with the Google account that administers your website's analytics.

  2. Choose the right property

    Use the account/property picker at the top left to select your website's GA4 property. Its ID is a number such as 438298996; older "UA-" properties no longer collect data.

  3. Open access management

    Click Admin (the gear icon, bottom left), then in the Property column choose:

    Admin › Property settings › Property access management
  4. Add our email

    Click the blue + button, choose Add users, and enter [agency email].

  5. Choose the role "Viewer"

    Select Viewer under "Standard roles". Viewer is read-only: we can see reports but cannot change settings, tracking or users. Leave "Notify new users by email" ticked.

  6. Click "Add"

    That's it. Let us know which property you added so we connect the right one.

Optional: help us measure leads accurately

If you want the dashboard to report leads (form submissions, calls, bookings), those actions must be marked as key events in GA4 (Admin › Data display › Key events). We can do this for you if you grant the Editor role instead. Key events only count from the day they are marked.

Can't see the Admin options?

You need the Administrator role on the property to add users. Ask whoever set up your analytics (often your web developer) to do this step, or to make you an Administrator first.

B
About 5 minutes

Google Search Console

Give us access to your website's search performance data.

  1. Open Search Console

    Go to search.google.com/search-console and sign in as an owner of your website's property.

  2. Select your website

    Pick your site from the property menu at the top left. If you see both a Domain property (e.g. example.com) and a URL-prefix property (e.g. https://www.example.com/), choose the Domain property: it covers all versions of your site.

  3. Open user management Settings (bottom of left menu) › Users and permissions
  4. Add our email

    Click Add user, enter [agency email], and set permission to Restricted. Restricted access can view all performance data but cannot change anything.

  5. Click "Add"

    Done. Search Console data is available immediately and includes up to 16 months of history.

Good to know

Search Console data is typically 2–3 days behind and Google hides very rare searches for privacy, so keyword tables never show 100% of clicks. Your dashboard notes this wherever it applies.

Don't see "Add user"?

Only a verified owner can add users. If your site isn't verified yet, Search Console will guide you through verification (usually a DNS record your domain provider or web developer can add).

Google Business Profile

Grant manager access and request Business Profile API access from Google.

Google requires an approved API access request before any software can read Business Profile performance data. There are two ways to do this. We recommend Option 1: you add us as a manager and we submit the request for you.

Recommended · ~5 min for you

Option 1: We handle the request

You complete steps C1 and C2 only. We submit Google's access request from our project and connect everything once approved.

~30–45 min for you

Option 2: You request access yourself

Use this if your company policy requires the API project to be owned by you. Complete C1, C2 and C3, then send us the credentials securely.

C1 · Check that your profile qualifies

Google only approves requests for profiles that meet these requirements:

  • The Business Profile is verified (it shows as verified in your Business Profile settings).
  • It has been active for at least 60 days.
  • It lists your website, and that website represents the business on the profile.
  • The profile is complete and up to date (hours, categories, contact details). This is recommended rather than strictly required.
Profile newer than 60 days, or not verified?

Complete verification first and let us know the date. We'll submit the request once the profile passes the 60-day mark; the rest of your dashboard works in the meantime.

C2 · Add us as a manager of your Business Profile

  1. Open your Business Profile

    Sign in to the Google account that owns the profile, then search Google for your business name, or open business.google.com and choose your business.

  2. Open the managers list ⋮ Menu › Business Profile settings › People and access (shown as Managers on some accounts)
  3. Add our email as a Manager

    Click Add, enter [agency email], choose the role Manager, then Invite. Google requires Manager (or Owner) access for the API request; we only read data and never edit your listing.

  4. Repeat for every location

    If you have several locations, add us to each one, or to the business group that contains them.

Option 1? You're finished.

Reply to let us know the invite is sent. We'll accept it, submit Google's "Application for Basic API Access", and confirm when Google approves. Skip C3.

C3
Option 2 only

Request API access yourself

Create a Google Cloud project, apply for access, then create sign-in credentials.

Step 1 · Create a Google Cloud project

  1. Open Google Cloud Console

    Go to console.cloud.google.com with the Google account that manages your Business Profile. Accept the terms if prompted.

  2. Create a project Project picker (top bar) › New project

    Name it e.g. SEO Dashboard - Business Profile and click Create.

  3. Copy the Project number

    On the project's Dashboard, the Project info card shows the Project number (digits only, e.g. 123456789012). You'll need it for the request.

Step 2 · Submit Google's access request

  1. Open the Business Profile API contact form

    Go to developers.google.com/my-business/content/prereqs and follow the link to the GBP API contact form.

  2. Choose "Application for Basic API Access"

    Fill in the form using an email address that is an owner or manager of the Business Profile. Include your Project number, business name and website.

  3. Wait for Google's email

    Google reviews the request and replies by email. Review times vary; plan for several days to a few weeks.

  4. Confirm approval Cloud Console › APIs & Services › search "Business Profile" › Quotas
    Quota shownMeaning
    0 requests per minuteNot approved yet. Keep waiting, or check the request details.
    300 requests per minuteApproved. Continue with Step 3.

Step 3 · Enable the APIs (after approval)

APIs & Services › Library then search for and Enable each of these:

APIUsed for
Business Profile Performance APIImpressions on Search and Maps, calls, website clicks, direction requests, search keywords
My Business Account Management APIListing your accounts and the locations we're allowed to read
My Business Business Information APILocation names, addresses, categories and website for the report

Some Business Profile APIs only appear in the Library after Google has approved your request.

Step 4 · Set up the Google sign-in screen

  1. Open the consent screen settings APIs & Services › OAuth consent screen (called Google Auth Platform in newer consoles)
  2. Fill in the app details

    User type External. App name SEO Dashboard, your support email, and your developer contact email.

  3. Add the scope

    Under Scopes / Data access, add https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage, the scope Google requires for Business Profile data.

  4. Add test users, then publish

    Add [agency email] as a test user. When we confirm the connection works, set the publishing status to In production. If Google asks for app verification, we'll support you through it.

Step 5 · Create the sign-in credentials

  1. Create an OAuth client APIs & Services › Credentials › Create credentials › OAuth client ID
  2. Choose "Web application"

    Name it SEO Dashboard. Under Authorised redirect URIs, add exactly the address we send you, for example https://[your-dashboard-domain]/api/integrations/gbp/callback.

  3. Send us the Client ID and Client secret securely

    Click Create. Share the Client ID and Client secret through a password manager share or another secure channel we agree on, never in a plain email or chat message.

That's everything.

Once we have the credentials, we'll sign in through Google's consent screen, select your locations, and your Business Profile chapter will start filling in.

Peace of mind

Security, privacy and frequently asked questions

Read-only use

The dashboard only reads reporting data. It never edits your website, analytics settings, search settings or Business Profile listing.

Secure sign-in

Connections use Google's official sign-in (OAuth). We never ask for your Google password, and access tokens are stored encrypted on our servers.

Your data stays separate

Each client's data is isolated at the database level. Only people you or we invite can see your dashboard.

You stay in control

You can remove our access at any time from the same screens used in this guide, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Frequently asked questions

QuestionAnswer
Will you be able to change my listing or website?The Business Profile request needs Manager access because that is what Google requires, but the dashboard only reads data. No changes are made through these connections.
How long does Business Profile approval take?It's decided by Google and varies. Plan for several days to a few weeks. Analytics and Search Console work in the meantime.
What if Google rejects the request?Usually the profile is under 60 days old, unverified, or missing a website. Fix that and the request can be resubmitted; we'll handle it under Option 1.
Is a "call click" a real phone call?No. Google reports taps on the call button, not completed calls. Your report labels it as "call clicks".
How far back does the data go?We import 90 days of history when we connect. Search Console keeps about 16 months; Business Profile metrics go back up to 18 months.
We have several locations or websites.Add us to each one (or the business group). The dashboard lets you view each location or site separately, or all combined.
